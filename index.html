Our Story

A farmhouse we couldn't bear to leave empty.

We bought this place for the porch and stayed for everything else — the wood stove that never quite goes out, the trail that starts behind the barn, the way the valley fills with fog most mornings until about nine.

We keep it small on purpose. Three rooms means we know your name by the time you've had your first coffee, and exactly how you take it by the second.
